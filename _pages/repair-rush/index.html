Support

Found a bug, a translation mistake or a level you can’t finish? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and the level number.

Report an ad

If you see an ad that is offensive or not suitable for the game, tap Settings → Report an ad, or email hezarfentech@gmail.com with what the ad showed and roughly when it appeared. You can also use the AdChoices icon on the ad itself to report it to Google.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

Are there in-app purchases? No. Repair Rush is free and supported by ads. Watching an ad for 2× coins is always optional.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device. It can’t be recovered after the app is deleted or the game is reset.

How do I change my ad choices? In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you can also change tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration? Open Settings from the home screen.
