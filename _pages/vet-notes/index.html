Support

Found a bug, a content error or a translation mistake? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app language and the note or question concerned.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress, bookmarks and quiz history are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. All notes, questions and calculators are inside the app. An internet connection is only used for ads.

Can I use the calculators on real patients? They are a study and quick-reference aid. Always check every result against current references and your own clinical judgement before treating an animal.

Why can I only answer a limited number of questions a day? The app is free and supported by ads. After the free questions for the day, you can watch a short ad to unlock more.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device and can’t be recovered after the app is deleted or its data is cleared.

How do I change the language? Open the Profile tab and choose Language.
