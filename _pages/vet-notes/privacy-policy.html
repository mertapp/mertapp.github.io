Vet Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vet Notes (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by Mert Korkmaz under the name MertApp (“we”, “us”). It applies to the App on iOS and Android.

In short: Vet Notes has no accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your reading progress, bookmarks, quiz history and settings stay on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may process device and ad-related data to deliver and measure those ads.

1. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device so it can work:

  • which notes you have read and which you have bookmarked,
  • your quiz answers, correct-answer count and daily study streak,
  • the number of quiz questions answered today and any extra questions unlocked with a rewarded ad,
  • settings (language and theme).

Values you type into the calculators (such as body weight or dose) are used only to show the result on screen and are not saved or sent anywhere. None of this information is sent to us. We have no servers that receive it. Deleting the App, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes it.

2. No analytics, notifications or accounts

The App does not use an analytics service, does not send push notifications and does not use Firebase or any other backend. All notes, quiz questions and translations are bundled inside the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Vet Notes is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The App shows banner ads, full-screen ads between some screens, and optional rewarded ads that unlock extra quiz questions; you never have to watch a rewarded ad. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or an app-specific identifier on iOS,
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location, such as country),
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version,
  • ad interaction data, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance of the ad SDK.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. It is not linked to your identity, and we do not receive information that identifies you personally. Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and on iOS it does not ask for permission to access Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • Ad personalization (Android): you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Ad personalization (iOS): you can limit ad tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and → Apple Advertising.
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: where the law requires your consent for ads, the App asks for it with Google’s consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Profile → Privacy choices.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all data collection by the App.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

Local storage of your progress, bookmarks and settings is necessary to provide the App you asked for. Personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and ad measurement are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

6. Children

Vet Notes is an educational app for veterinary students, professionals and animal-health learners. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and security

Because we do not run servers for the App, we do not store your data. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or delete the App. Ad traffic is sent over encrypted connections, and data processed by Google is kept in line with Google’s own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey’s KVKK, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are handled through your device settings or directly with Google. You may still contact us and we will help, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Third-party links

Ads in the App may lead to websites or apps we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App’s store listing release notes.
