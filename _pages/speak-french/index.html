French for real life

Order at a café, check into a hotel, ask for directions or practise a job interview. Work through practical roleplay goals at your own pace.

Learn while you talk

Receive French replies, grammar corrections, explanations and suggested responses. Review saved corrections before your next conversation.

Listen and speak

Use voice input and hear French replies aloud. Daily vocabulary and grammar tips help you keep a steady learning habit.

Start at your level

Choose your learning goals, French level and one of 52 interface language options. Version 1.0.5 improves conversation context and recovery from connection errors.

Free practice and optional Premium

Start with up to six successful AI replies each day. Premium provides more conversation practice and access to all roleplay scenarios. Weekly and monthly plans are offered; current prices and any eligible trial are shown in the app before purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings, or restore purchases from your app profile.

AI features are intended for adults aged 18 or older and require an internet connection. AI can make mistakes. Voice input requires microphone and speech permissions. Message sharing starts after your AI consent.
