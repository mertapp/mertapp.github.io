Home Cleaning Schedule

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Home Cleaning Schedule (the “App”) handles information. Mert Korkmaz publishes the App under the name MertApp (“we”, “us”). The policy covers the App on iOS and Android.

In short: Home Cleaning Schedule has no accounts, no sign-up and no in-app purchases. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your rooms, tasks and cleaning history stay on your device. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may process device and ad-related data to deliver and measure those ads.

1. Information stored only on your device

The App saves the following locally on your device so it can work:

  • the rooms you set up, your cleaning tasks (including tasks you add, edit or hide) and their schedule,
  • your cleaning history: completed and skipped tasks, daily plans, rest days and streaks,
  • your settings: cleaning days, daily target, reminder time, theme, language and seasonal packs.

This information is never sent to us. We have no servers that receive it. You can remove it at any time with Settings → Reset all data, or by deleting the App.

2. Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. Scheduling it does not involve any server, and the notification content never leaves your device. You can turn reminders off in Settings → Reminders or in your device’s notification settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Home Cleaning Schedule is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location, such as country),
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version,
  • ad interaction data, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance of the ad SDK.

On iOS the App does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is not made available to ads through the App, and the App does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. Google’s use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: where the law requires your consent for ads, the App asks for it with a consent message before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options. Every feature of the App keeps working whatever you choose.
  • Ad personalization (Android): you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Your cleaning data: delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all data.
  • Uninstalling the App stops all data collection by the App.

5. Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

Local storage of your rooms, tasks and settings is necessary to provide the cleaning planner you asked for. Personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Non-personalized advertising, fraud prevention and ad measurement are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the App free.

6. Children

Home Cleaning Schedule is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and security

Because we do not run servers for the App, we do not store your data. Data on your device stays there until you reset it in the App or delete the App. Data processed by Google is kept in line with Google’s own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Turkey’s KVKK, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are handled in the App, through your device settings or directly with Google. You may still contact us and we will help, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Third-party links

Ads may lead to websites or apps we do not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the App’s store listing release notes.
