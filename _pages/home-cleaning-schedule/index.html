Found a bug, a translation error or a task that doesn’t fit? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version and the app language.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and everything is saved on your device.

Is it really free? Yes. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. The app is supported by ads.

How is my daily plan chosen? Tasks that are due or overdue are picked first, by priority, until your daily target is reached. Deep-cleaning tasks wait until everyday work is covered.

I don’t clean every day. Can I skip days? Yes. Pick your cleaning days in Settings → Cleaning days, or mark a single day as a rest day.

The reminder doesn’t arrive. Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your device settings, and that the daily reminder is on in Settings → Reminders.

How do I start over? Use Settings → Reset all data. This deletes your rooms, tasks and history from the device.
