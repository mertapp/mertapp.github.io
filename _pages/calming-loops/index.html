Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the game language and the level number.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. Every level is inside the game. An internet connection is only used for ads.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every Journey level is checked by a solver before it ships. If you get stuck, use a hint or restart the level.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device and can’t be recovered after the game is deleted or Reset progress is used.

The animations are too much. Turn on Reduce motion in Settings. The game also follows your device’s reduce-motion setting.

How do I change the language? Open Settings and choose Language.
