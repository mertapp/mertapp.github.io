YogaMood

YogaMood Support

Help with your practice, account, reminders, progress, and Premium subscription.

Choosing a practice

Choose how you feel on Home. YogaMood recommends a flow using your mood, primary goal, and preferred duration. You can also browse every available practice in Library.

Practicing safely

Move within a comfortable range and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, numbness, or unusual shortness of breath. YogaMood supports general wellness and does not replace medical care.

Progress and sync

Guest progress is stored on the device. When you sign in, your profile, sessions, journal entries, program progress, and favorites can also sync through Firebase.

Restore or cancel Premium

Open Premium from Profile and choose Restore Purchases to recover an eligible subscription. Choose Manage Subscription to change or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account.

Delete an account

Open Settings and choose Delete Account. Confirm your password or reauthenticate with the sign-in provider shown on screen to remove your account and known synced data. Deleting the YogaMood account does not cancel a store subscription. You can also review the web deletion instructions or submit a request by email.

Contact support
