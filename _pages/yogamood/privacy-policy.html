YogaMood

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what YogaMood collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect account details such as name and email, practice preferences, mood scores, session history, optional journal notes and reflection tags, favorites, program progress, reminder settings, subscription status, AI Coach usage counts, and basic app interaction or diagnostic data.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, personalize recommendations, provide requested AI guidance, sync progress, provide subscriptions, calculate insights, deliver requested reminders, support users, protect the service, and understand which product experiences are useful.

AI Practice Coach

Before each AI Coach request, we ask permission to send your current mood, practice preferences (goal, level, duration, and time), app language, and optional question through Firebase to OpenAI for guidance and content safety checks. Generated answers are also checked by OpenAI before display. Your name, email, journal notes, and session history are not sent to OpenAI. You can decline each request and keep using other YogaMood features. Avoid including sensitive or identifying information in questions. Questions and answers are not stored in Firestore unless you report an answer: the reported answer is stored with your account for safety review until removed or your account is deleted. Monthly request counts and daily report counts are stored to enforce limits. OpenAI processes submitted information under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Service providers

YogaMood uses Firebase for authentication, cloud data, server functions, diagnostics, and analytics; RevenueCat to manage purchase entitlements and subscription status; and OpenAI to generate optional Premium AI Coach guidance. Apple or Google processes store payments. These providers handle data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and retention

Guest data remains on the device unless the app is removed or its data is cleared. Signed-in data may remain in our cloud systems while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward when needed for security, legal, or operational purposes.

Your choices

You can edit preferences, disable reminders, avoid optional journal notes, sign out, or request access, correction, export, or deletion of account data. The in-app Delete Account action removes your known synced profile and practice records. You can also use the web deletion instructions. Store subscriptions must be managed through Apple or Google.

Children

YogaMood is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access rules for cloud data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. A revised effective date will appear here. Contact support with privacy questions or requests.
