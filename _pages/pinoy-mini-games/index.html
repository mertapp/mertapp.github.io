Support

Found a wrong quiz answer, a bug or a translation mistake? Email hezarfendigital@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version (shown in Settings) and, for the quiz, the category and the question.

FAQ

Do I need an account or internet? No. There is no sign-up, and every game works offline. Ads only load when your phone is online.

I used to sign in to Pinoy Quiz. Where is my account? The new version no longer uses accounts. Your stats and unlocked themes are saved on your device.

How do I unlock themes? Open Themes and watch a short, optional ad. Unlocked themes stay on your device.

How do I get fewer ads? In Settings you can watch one optional ad for a 30-minute break from full-screen ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Open Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). On iPhone you can also use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration? Open Settings from the home screen.
