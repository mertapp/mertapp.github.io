Support

Found a mistake in a recipe, a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app language and the recipe name.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your favorites, list and plan are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. All recipes are inside the app. An internet connection is only used for ads.

I lost my favorites or shopping list. They live only on the device and can’t be recovered after the app is deleted.

How do I change the language or units? Open Settings from the top of the Discover screen.

Are the calories exact? No. Nutrition values are estimates. The app is not medical advice.
