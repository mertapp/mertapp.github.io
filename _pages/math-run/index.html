Support

Found a wrong answer, a bug or a translation mistake? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version (shown in Settings) and the world and level.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device. It can’t be recovered after the app is deleted or the game is reset.

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Open Settings with the gear icon on the home screen.
