Support

Found a mistake in a question or rationale, a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the exam, the app language and the question.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

Is it really free? Yes. There are no subscriptions and no daily question limit. The app is supported by ads.

Are these real exam questions? No. All questions are original practice items. The app is not affiliated with NCSBN, the NMC or the PRC.

Who wrote the questions? They are drafted with AI assistance and checked automatically, but not yet reviewed by a clinician. The app labels them this way.

Can I keep or remove my progress? Use Profile → Export my data to save a copy, or Profile → Delete my data to remove it.
