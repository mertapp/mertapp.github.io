Support

Found a wrong result, a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app language, the tool you used and the values you entered.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up. Your projects, history and settings are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. Every calculator works without an internet connection. The connection is only used for ads.

Where do the prices come from? You enter them. The app does not ship any built-in material prices, because prices differ by country, city and supplier. Tap Add material price in a tool to see a cost estimate.

How do I save a result to a project? Calculate first. Save to project appears under the result, next to Share and Copy.

How do I move my projects to a new phone? Open Settings → Export backup on the old phone, keep the file, then use Settings → Restore backup on the new one.

How do I change the language, units or currency? Open Settings.
