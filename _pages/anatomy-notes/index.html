Support

Found a mistake in a note or question, a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app language and the topic or question.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. All notes, quizzes and flashcards are inside the app. An internet connection is only used for ads.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device. It can’t be recovered after the app is deleted or progress is reset.

How do I change the language? Open Progress → Settings → Language.
