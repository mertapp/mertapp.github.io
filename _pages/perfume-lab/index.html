Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug or a translation error? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the game language and the level number.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress and Library are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes. All levels are inside the game. An internet connection is only used for ads.

Is every level solvable? Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before it ships. If you get stuck, use undo or restart the level.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device and can’t be recovered after the game is deleted or Reset progress is used.

The colours are hard to tell apart. Turn on colourblind mode in Settings. It adds a family symbol to every bottle label.

How do I change the language? Open Settings and choose Language.
