AI Speak Spanish · MertApp

Spanish practice, at your pace.

AI Speak Spanish: Practice Me helps adults practise Spanish conversations with fictional AI tutors, real-life roleplays, grammar corrections and saved review cards. Choose your level, learning goal and one of 53 interface languages.

Free practice and Premium

Start with four tutors, three roleplays and up to six free messages a day. Optional weekly and monthly Premium subscriptions unlock more conversation practice, all roleplays, voice input and smart flashcards. Store prices, billing periods and eligible trial terms are shown before purchase. Online AI is subject to service availability and fair-use limits, currently up to 10 requests per minute and 300 per day per account, with shared network and service capacity limits.

AI features are intended for adults aged 18 or older. AI can make mistakes. An internet connection and explicit AI data-sharing consent are required for online replies. Microphone and speech recognition permissions are requested when you choose voice input.

Contact support

For questions about the app, technical problems or Premium access, email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Include your app version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or confidential conversation content.

Subscriptions and data

Restore eligible purchases from the Premium screen using the same Apple account. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Uninstalling the app or deleting your app account does not cancel a subscription.

Delete your app account and learning data from Profile → Delete Account. An internet connection is needed to delete cloud records. If deletion fails, reconnect and retry. Anonymous account recovery after reinstalling or changing devices is not guaranteed.
