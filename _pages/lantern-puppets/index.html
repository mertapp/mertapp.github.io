Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug or a translation mistake? Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the app version (shown in Settings) and the level number.

FAQ

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up, and your progress is saved on your device.

What are the candles? Every piece you place burns one candle. Lifting a piece back is free. If the candles run out, you can retry the level or watch an ad for three more candles.

Can I buy coins or hints? No. There are no in-app purchases. You earn coins by finishing levels, and you can watch an optional ad for extra rewards.

I lost my progress. Progress lives only on the device. It can’t be recovered after the app is deleted or the game is reset.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration? Open Settings from the home screen.
