Support

Email hezarfentech@gmail.com. For technical issues, include your device model, operating system, App version, language and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid including sensitive wellness records unless needed.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. History and preferences are saved on your device.

Does it work offline? Bundled routines do. Voice guidance needs an installed system voice in your language. Ads and external links need a connection.

How can I transfer progress? In Profile, export a backup on your old device, then restore that file on your new device. Keep the file private; it includes check-ins.

How can I delete history? Use Reset local data in Profile. This retains language/theme, audio and reminder preferences. Delete exported copies and device backups separately.

How do I change advertising choices? Open the advertising privacy row in Profile when available, or use your device's tracking and advertising settings.

Is this medical care? No. The App is for general wellness and does not diagnose, treat or rehabilitate medical conditions. Stop if movement causes pain, dizziness, numbness or unusual discomfort.
